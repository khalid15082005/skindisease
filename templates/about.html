About Skin Disease AI

🔬 What is Skin Disease AI?

Skin Disease AI is an advanced machine learning application designed to assist in the diagnosis of various skin conditions. Our AI system can analyze skin lesion images and provide predictions for 6 different types of skin conditions with high accuracy.

🎯 Supported Conditions

  • Acne - Common skin condition with pimples
  • Carcinoma - Skin cancer
  • Eczema - Inflammatory skin condition
  • Keratosis - Rough, scaly patches
  • Millia - Small white bumps
  • Rosacea - Facial redness and bumps
📊 Model Information
  • Architecture: Xception
  • Accuracy: 92%
  • Training Data: 1,657 images
  • Classes: 6 skin conditions
⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This tool is for educational purposes only. Always consult a qualified dermatologist for medical diagnosis and treatment.

The AI predictions should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice.
